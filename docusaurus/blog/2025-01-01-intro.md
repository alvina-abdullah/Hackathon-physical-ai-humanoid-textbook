# Introduction to Physical AI Humanoid Development

Welcome to the Physical AI Humanoid Textbook project! This comprehensive guide is designed to help you understand and develop AI-powered humanoid robots using state-of-the-art techniques and best practices.

## What You'll Learn

In this textbook, we cover four essential modules that form the foundation of humanoid robotics:

1. **AI Systems Introduction**: Fundamental AI concepts tailored for humanoid applications
2. **ROS2 Humanoid Control**: Advanced control systems using the Robot Operating System
3. **Digital Twin Simulation**: Safe development through simulation and digital twins
4. **AI Robot Brain**: Cognitive architectures and decision-making systems

## Getting Started

To begin your journey in humanoid robotics development:

1. Set up your development environment with ROS2
2. Understand the core concepts in AI systems
3. Practice with simulation environments before moving to physical robots
4. Gradually build complexity in your robotic applications

## The Future of Humanoid Robotics

Humanoid robots represent a fascinating intersection of artificial intelligence, mechanical engineering, and cognitive science. As these systems become more sophisticated, they will play increasingly important roles in healthcare, education, manufacturing, and daily life assistance.
